<h1>🚫 Ghostbusters Tactical Soundboard (<code>gb-soundboard</code>)</h1>

<p>An ad-free, open-source tactical audio deck designed specifically for running <b>Savage Ghostbusters</b> and tabletop roleplaying game (TTRPG) sessions.</p>

<p>🌐 <b>Live Soundboard App</b>: <a href="https://angrystatic.github.io/gb-soundboard/">https://angrystatic.github.io/gb-soundboard/</a></p>

<hr>

<h2>🎲 Project Overview</h2>

<p>Running paranormal investigations at the tabletop requires quick access to atmospheric sounds, tactical gear cues, and dynamic symphonic stingers without wading through third-party apps or intrusive advertisements.</p>

<p>This repository hosts both the raw audio assets and the web interface for a responsive soundboard that can be opened in a dedicated browser tab, stream on a second monitor, or run on a tablet next to your GM notes.</p>

<hr>

<h2>🎧 Audio Catalog &amp; Folder Structure</h2>

<p>The sound library is organized into six phase-based categories that mirror a Ghostbusters session's narrative arc:</p>

<h3>🔊 1. Ambient SFX (<code>/sounds/Ambient SFX/</code>)</h3>
<p>Background environmental loops, room tones, muffled TV static behind doors, gramophone filters, ticking clocks, and distant thunder to set localized scene atmosphere.</p>

<h3>📞 2. Call to Arms SFX (<code>/sounds/Call to Arms SFX/</code>)</h3>
<p>Emergency deployment cues including:</p>
<ul>
  <li>☎️ Dispatch phone rings &amp; computer terminal alerts.</li>
  <li>🚨 Ecto-1 sirens (Engine Start/Drive, Approach &amp; Stop).</li>
  <li>🚨 Firehouse alarms (with and without full bell runs).</li>
  <li>📺 Retro 80s TV &amp; radio commercial jingles.</li>
</ul>

<h3>📟 3. Investigation SFX (<code>/sounds/Investigation SFX/</code>)</h3>
<p>Paranormal search &amp; sensory gear audio:</p>
<ul>
  <li>📟 <b>P.K.E. Meter</b>: Continuous scanning chirps and target lock-on chirps.</li>
  <li>🕶️ <b>Ecto Goggles</b>: High-frequency activation whine and POV focus clicks.</li>
  <li>🖥️ <b>Firehouse Mainframe</b>: Ambient computer terminal hums and data processing sounds.</li>
  <li>🌬️ Cold spot whooshes and atmospheric ghost shifts.</li>
</ul>

<h3>⚡ 4. Combat &amp; Containment SFX (<code>/sounds/Combat &amp; Containment SFX/</code>)</h3>
<p>Tactical equipment and capture hardware:</p>
<ul>
  <li>🎒 <b>Proton Pack</b>: Startup sequences, stream firing (local grain variations), heat accumulation, venting, and overheat alerts.</li>
  <li>🏺 <b>Ghost Traps</b>: Deployment slide/throw, suction vortex capture loops, full trap warning beeps, and mechanical snap closures.</li>
  <li>🧪 <b>Slime Blower</b>: Chemical spray chugs, tank pumps, and squelch splats.</li>
  <li>🔋 <b>Containment Vault</b>: Underground grid hums, thrumming power-ups, and catastrophic breach explosions.</li>
</ul>

<h3>🎲 5. Meta Game Music SFX (<code>/sounds/Meta Game Music SFX/</code>)</h3>
<p>Tabletop mechanics and comedic table feedback:</p>
<ul>
  <li>🎰 <b>Joker Jackpot</b>: Wild Card raise reward cue.</li>
  <li>💎 <b>Benny Spend</b>: Token spend / reroll confirmation chime.</li>
  <li>🥴 <b>Shaken / Stunned</b>: Tactical status stingers.</li>
  <li>📻 <b>Walkie Talkie</b>: Radio squawks and squelch clicks.</li>
  <li>😱 <b>Wilhelm Scream</b>: Classic comedic character falls.</li>
</ul>

<h3>🎭 6. Moods Music &amp; Score (<code>/sounds/Moods Music/</code>)</h3>
<p>Symphonic score loops and cinematic accents split across specific dramatic hooks:</p>

<ul>
  <li>🎺 <b>Big Reveal</b>: Minor-key horn swells, brass hits, and cymbal strikes.</li>
  <li>🐝 <b>Bureaucratic Buzz</b>: Walter Peck annoyance themes (piano walk to tuba walk).</li>
  <li>⚔️ <b>Chaotic Kinetic</b>: High-energy combat music loops and battle drum tracks.</li>
  <li>🎷 <b>Comedic Relief</b>: Tuba walk variations for blunders and clumsy PC maneuvers.</li>
  <li>👁️ <b>Creeping Dread Eerie</b>: Slow tension-building woodwinds, cemetery underground drones, and library ambiance.</li>
  <li>💡 <b>Egon's Eureka</b>: Uplifting flute/string cues for scientific breakthroughs.</li>
  <li>🔮 <b>Occult Mystery</b>: Suspenseful 13th-floor mirror reveals and occult ambiance.</li>
  <li>⚡ <b>Stingers</b>: Over 50 rapid symphonic jump-scares, hit cues, and dramatic stingers.</li>
</ul>
